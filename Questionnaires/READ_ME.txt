These files contain data reported in:

Andrade, J., May, J., Deeprose, C., Baugh, S.-J. & Ganis, G. (2013) Assessing Vividness of Mental Imagery-  The Plymouth Sensory Imagery Questionnaire. British Journal of Psychology, 105, 547-563.

DOI:10.1111/bjop.12050

PsiQStudy1N_404.csv - STUDY 1: EXPLORATORY FACTOR ANALYSIS

PsiQStudy2N_223.csv - full data from STUDY 2: CONFIRMATORY FACTOR ANALYSIS
PsiQStudy2N_209.csv - analysed data from STUDY 2: CONFIRMATORY FACTOR ANALYSIS excluding 12 participants who had done Study 1 and 2 who gave the maximum score (7) for all PsiQ items
PsiQStudy2N_41.csv - test-retest data from 41 participants

PsiQStudy3N_212.csv - full data from STUDY 3: EXTERNAL VALIDITY

The similarly named files with the suffix Codebook describe the contents of each column
